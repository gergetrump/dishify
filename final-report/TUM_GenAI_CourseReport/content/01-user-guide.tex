\section{What is Dishify?}
Dishify is an AI-powered cooking assistant designed to eliminate mealtime fatigue.
If you're too tired or uninspired to figure out what to make, Dishify does the
thinking for you. Simply input the ingredients you already have at home via typing,
speaking, or photographing your fridge, and Dishify instantly generates delicious,
tailored recipes – helping you save time, reduce food waste, and make the most of
your pantry.

Dishify is built for English-speaking everyday home cooks who own food but not a plan,
and who lose more time to deciding than to cooking. It respects your eating preferences
and recipes that clash with a stored allergy or diet are removed before you ever see
them.

\section{Getting Started}
\begin{center}
    \includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{./Ressourcen/user-guide/landing-page.png}
    \captionof{figure}{The Dishify landing page.}
    \label{fig:ug-landing-page}
\end{center}

Dishify runs on the web today; a native iOS app is also implemented and can be
run locally from source (not yet on the App Store). Getting your first recipe 
suggestion takes four quick steps:



\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=1pt, topsep=3pt]
    \item \textbf{Open Dishify} at \url{http://167.233.165.44} — no installation,
        works in any modern browser.
    \item \textbf{Sign up} with a username, email, and password to create your
        account.
    \item \textbf{Set what to avoid} – allergies, diets, or ingredients you'd 
        rather skip – so Dishify never suggests around them.

    \item \textbf{Add what you have} and hit Find my recipes.
\end{enumerate}

Within seconds, your pantry becomes a ranked list of recipes made for you.



\section{Features}

\subsection{Dietary, Allergy, and Restriction Filtering}
\begin{center}
    \includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{./Ressourcen/user-guide/preferences.png}
    \captionof{figure}{The preferences page.}
    \label{fig:ug-preferences}
\end{center}

No more scanning every recipe for hidden allergens or off-limits ingredients — Dishify
does it for you. Choose from 72 tags across allergies, diets, religious and ethical
rules, medical needs, and ingredient exclusions. These aren't hints: a recipe that
conflicts with your preferences is removed before it ever reaches your results, so
you never have to double-check. Set it once, and it applies to every search from
then on.



\subsection{Pantry-based Recipe Recommendation}
\begin{center}
    \includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{./Ressourcen/user-guide/ingredients.png}
    \captionof{figure}{The ingredients page.}
    \label{fig:ug-ingredients}
\end{center}

Tell Dishify what's already in your kitchen — down to the exact quantity and unit,
like 2 eggs. Dishify searches a library of roughly 2.2 million recipes to find the
ones that fit both your pantry and your mood. Craving something specific? Add an
eating vibe like "fast" and Dishify tailors its suggestions accordingly.

\begin{center}
    \includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{./Ressourcen/user-guide/voice.png}

\begin{center}

Hands full while unpacking groceries? Just say what you have out loud, and Dishify adds it
straight to your pantry — no typing required. Or skip manual entry altogether: snap a photo
of your fridge or pantry and Dishify recognizes what's there automatically. Either way,
detected items appear for you to confirm before anything is searched, so a misheard word
never silently changes your results.

Every suggestion comes ranked by a relevance score, so the best match for your pantry and
your cravings rises to the top. At a glance, you can see exactly which ingredients you already
have and which ones you'd still need to pick up — no guesswork required.
